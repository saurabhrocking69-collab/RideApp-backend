Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Applies to the Sppero rider app and the Sppero Buddy driver app

This policy explains what information Sppero ("we", "us", "the platform") collects from riders and driver-partners who use our mobile apps, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Sppero is a ride-hailing marketplace connecting riders with independent driver-partners for auto, bike, car, e-rickshaw, intercity and hourly-rental rides across our operating cities in India.

2. Location Data — details

Location is core to how Sppero works:

  • Riders: location is used to show nearby drivers, set pickup points and calculate fares. Collected only while the app is open.
  • Driver-partners: location is collected in the foreground always, and in the background only while you are marked "Online", so riders can see your live position and be matched with you. Background location stops the moment you go offline.
  • Location history tied to a trip is retained for support, safety investigations, and fraud checks; it is not used for advertising.

3. Who We Share Data With

  • The other party in a trip — a rider and their matched driver-partner can see each other's name, phone number, live location and vehicle details for the duration of that trip, so the ride can happen.
  • Razorpay (payment gateway) — processes online payments. We share only what's needed to complete a transaction.
  • Google Maps Platform — powers maps, place search and route/ETA calculation.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers push notifications to your device.
  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of riders, drivers or the public.

4. Data Retention

We keep trip, payment and account records for as long as your account is active and for a reasonable period after, to meet legal/tax record-keeping obligations, resolve disputes and prevent fraud. You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time (see Section 6) — some trip records may be retained in anonymised or minimal form where required by law.

5. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Access to production data is restricted to authorised personnel only. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • Access/Correct: update your name and other profile details from within the app.
  • Location permission: can be revoked anytime from your phone's Settings — the app just won't be able to find rides for you until re-enabled.
  • Notifications: can be turned off from your phone's Settings.
  • Delete your account/data: email help@sppero.in from your registered contact details with a deletion request. We'll confirm and process it, subject to any records we're legally required to retain.

7. Children's Privacy

Sppero is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Write to help@sppero.in.
